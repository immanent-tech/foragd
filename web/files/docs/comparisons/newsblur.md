## Key Differences

Foragd is a simpler, single-price alternative to NewsBlur for readers who want every feature included from day one,
without a tier ladder to climb.

Foragd and NewsBlur are both hosted feed readers built around a calm, no-ads, no-algorithm reading philosophy. They are
closer to each other in spirit than either is to Feedly or Inoreader. But they take very different approaches to pricing
and personalization. NewsBlur splits its product across four tiers: Free, Premium ($36/yr), Premium Archive ($99/yr),
and Premium Pro ($29/mo) and features like full story archives, Ask AI, and story deduplication locked behind the two
higher tiers. Foragd is **one plan**, $5/month or $36/year (currently on sale), **with everything included**: semantic
search, unlimited saved searches, newsletters, YouTube, subreddits, and Google News subscriptions.

NewsBlur's entry paid tier is the same $36/year as Foragd's, but caps subscriptions at 1,024 sites versus Foragd's
3,000, and reserves its most powerful features (full searchable archive, AI-powered story summaries, and duplicate story
clustering) for the $99/year Archive tier or $29/month Pro tier. Foragd includes semantic search, saved search
subscriptions, and multi-source aggregation at its only price point. This page breaks down the key differences to help
you decide which is the better fit.

## Equivalent Features and Substitutes

### Intelligence Training (NewsBlur) vs. Semantic Search (Foragd)

NewsBlur's signature feature is Intelligence Training: you mark stories, authors, tags, and titles you like or dislike,
and over time NewsBlur learns to highlight what matters to you and hide what doesn't. It's a well-regarded feature with
over a decade of refinement behind it, and it rewards ongoing use. The more you train it, the better it gets.

Foragd doesn't have a learned classifier. Instead, it leans on full-text and semantic search: rather than training the
system over weeks to surface what you want, you search for it directly, and semantic search can match on meaning and
topic rather than exact keywords. Saved search subscriptions let you turn any search into a standing subscription that
keeps populating with new matches. The trade-off is philosophical as much as technical; NewsBlur asks you to teach it
your taste over time, Foragd asks you to describe what you want in the moment.

### Web Feeds (NewsBlur) vs. Subreddits and Google News Search (Foragd)

NewsBlur's Web Feeds feature lets you follow any website as a feed, even if it doesn't publish RSS, by monitoring the
page for changes. This is broad and flexible for niche or RSS-less sites.

Foragd takes a narrower but more purpose-built approach: subreddit subscriptions and Google News search subscriptions
are first-class, no-configuration source types. If what you're after is a subreddit or a standing news search rather
than an arbitrary website, Foragd gets you there in a couple of clicks rather than configuring a generic page-monitoring
feed.

Additionally Foragd support entitles you to ask for custom feeds for a site without one; contact support and we'll see
if we can create a custom feed subscription for any site you want. This means you don't need to be a frontend developer
or understand how web pages work to get their content; just ask us and we'll do the hard work for you.

### Tiered Feature Gating (NewsBlur) vs. Single Plan (Foragd)

NewsBlur's most powerful capabilities are a full searchable story archive, Ask AI, Daily Briefing, and story
deduplication. These are only available on the $99/year Archive tier or $29/month Pro tier. Regex-based training is
Pro-only. Getting the full NewsBlur experience means climbing past the $36/year Premium tier.

Foragd has no equivalent ladder. Every feature (semantic search, saved searches, email newsletters, YouTube, subreddits,
Google News) is included at the single $5/month price. There's no tier to grow into and no feature to discover is
locked behind a plan you're not on.

## Which RSS Reader Should You Choose: Foragd or NewsBlur?

NewsBlur is a strong choice if you want a free way to start reading feeds today, or if you're drawn to Intelligence
Training as a way to teach a reader your taste over time. Its native iOS and Android apps, broad third-party client
support, and AI-powered Ask AI and Daily Briefing features (once you're on the Archive tier or above) make it a
genuinely feature-rich product with over a decade of track record behind it. If you want the deepest feature set
NewsBlur offers, though, expect to pay $99/year or $29/month to unlock the archive, AI features, and deduplication.

Foragd is the better choice if you want everything included at one predictable price, without deciding which tier
actually has the feature you need. At $5/month, you get semantic search, unlimited saved searches, 50 newsletters via a
masked email address, and built-in subreddit and Google News search subscriptions. You don't need to upgrade to get more
features. Foragd doesn't currently offer a native iOS app; the responsive website is designed to work on all devices and
all browsers. Foragd does not have third-party client support, and it doesn't have a learned personalization engine like
Intelligence Training, so if either of those matters most to you, NewsBlur may be the better fit.

If you're already using NewsBlur and want to try Foragd's single-tier approach, switching takes a couple of minutes:
export your OPML file from NewsBlur and import it into Foragd, and every feed you follow will be waiting for you.

## How to Switch from NewsBlur to Foragd

Switching from NewsBlur to Foragd takes a couple of minutes and requires no manual re-subscribing. Every feed you
currently follow in NewsBlur can be moved to Foragd in a single file import.

### 1. Export your feeds from NewsBlur

Log into your NewsBlur account and go to your account settings. Look for the option to export your subscriptions as OPML
(NewsBlur also automatically emails you an OPML backup whenever you make significant changes to your feeds), so you may
already have a recent one in your inbox. Download the file.

### 2. Import into Foragd

Log into your Foragd account and go to Settings, then Import. Select your OPML file and confirm the import. Foragd will
read every feed in the file and add them to your subscriptions automatically, preserving your NewsBlur folder structure
as Foragd collections. Depending on the number of feeds, this typically completes in under a minute.

### 3. You're done

Every feed you followed in NewsBlur is now in Foragd, and new articles will start appearing as they're fetched. Your
NewsBlur account remains untouched, so you can keep both running in parallel until you're comfortable with the switch,
or downgrade to NewsBlur's free tier if you want to keep it as a backup.
