## Key Differences

Foragd is a cheaper, higher-limit alternative to Feedly for independent readers who want RSS, newsletter, and search
subscriptions in one place without per-feature caps.

Foragd and Feedly are both web-based RSS feed readers, but they differ significantly in pricing structure, feed limits,
and philosophy. Foragd offers a single plan with 3000 feed subscriptions and built-in email newsletter support starting
at USD $4.92/month billed annually. Feedly's base plan at $6.99/month with a 1000 feed limit and no newsletter support.
This page further breaks down the key differences to help you choose.

## Which RSS Reader Should You Choose: Foragd or Feedly?

Foragd is the stronger choice for most independent readers. At $4.92/month billed annually, it undercuts Feedly's base
plan while offering three times the feed limit (3,000 subscriptions versus Feedly's 1,000) in addition to email
newsletter support and remote article fetching that Feedly doesn't include at any base tier.

Feedly has broader name recognition and native mobile apps, but Foragd's fully responsive web app works on every device
without an install, and its single flat-rate plan means you won't hit a paywall as your reading list grows.

If you're an independent reader, blogger, researcher, or journalist who wants to follow a large number of sources across
websites and newsletters without paying for features that should be standard, Foragd delivers more at a lower price. If
you're already a Feedly user, switching takes under two minutes — export your OPML from Feedly's settings, import it
into Foragd, and your entire reading list carries over instantly.

## How to Switch from Feedly to Foragd

Switching from Feedly to Foragd takes about two minutes and requires no manual work. Every feed you currently follow
in Feedly can be moved to Foragd in a single file import.

### 1. Export your feeds from Feedly

Log into Feedly and go to your profile settings. Under the "Organize" section, look for the OPML export option. Feedly
will generate a single `.opml` file containing every feed you currently subscribe to, organized into whatever folders
you've created. Download this file — it's yours to keep regardless of what you do next.

### 2. Import into Foragd

Log into your Foragd account and go to Settings, then Import. Select your OPML file and confirm the import. Foragd will
read every feed in the file and add them to your subscriptions automatically, preserving your folder structure as
categories. Depending on the number of feeds, this typically completes in under a minute.

### 3. You're done!

Every feed you followed in Feedly is now in Foragd. New articles will start appearing as feeds are fetched. Your
Feedly account remains untouched, you still need to cancel any existing subscription. As such, you can move back to
it at any time, or, keep both running in parallel until you're comfortable with the switch.
