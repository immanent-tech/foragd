A quick update post summarizing the updates in [Foragd](https://foragd.app) for June 2026.

**What’s new in this update:**

- **Better Source Integration:** It is now much easier to add YouTube channels (via `@name`) or search through channels
  by text.
- **Google News Feeds:** You can now add Google news searches as a feed. For example, using `24h site:reuters.com` will
  generate a live feed of only the articles from the last 24 hours from `reuters.com`.
- **Medium**: Foragd is now better at finding the feeds for medium.com sources, including ones on a custom domain (i.e.,
  not `medium.com`).
- **Bulk Management:** I’ve added a dedicated page in the settings for quick management of your subscriptions.

**UX & UI Improvements:**

- **Masonry Layout:** On desktop, the subscriptions, and articles now use a “masonry” grid for a more organized, visually
  pleasing layout.
- **Accessibility:** I’m continuing to work on accessibility best practices and adding more descriptive tooltips.

**Mobile:** I’ve just released an Android app for Foragd (currently in early access). I’d love to get some feedback on
the mobile experience!
