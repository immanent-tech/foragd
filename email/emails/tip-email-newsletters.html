Did you know you can get your email newsletters delivered to Foragd. This has many advantages:

Protect your identity.

Foragd generates a unique masked email for you to use.

Keep organised.

Manage your email newsletters like your feed subscriptions.

Filter and Search.

Filter emails and use full-text search on their contents to cut through the noise.

Get started:

  1. Go to your Settings->Account tab.
  2. Scroll down to Subscribe to Email Newsletters.
  3. Click the Generate email address button.
  4. Copy the email that is displayed.

Use this email when subscribing to newsletters and get all the benefits of content management in Foragd and protect your own email.
